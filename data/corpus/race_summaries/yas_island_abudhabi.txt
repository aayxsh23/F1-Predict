Yas Marina Circuit (Yas Island, Abu Dhabi) — Circuit Notes

Yas Marina hosts the traditional season finale and underwent a significant 2021 reprofiling (several corners in the final sector were reshaped and widened specifically to improve overtaking, after the circuit had developed a reputation as one of the hardest to pass at on the calendar). Since that change, overtaking_difficulty is rated at a moderate level (≈0.55) — a meaningful improvement on the old layout's reputation, though still tighter than a genuine power circuit like Monza or Bahrain.

DNF rate is among the lowest in the reference table (≈0.08) — the circuit has generous, modern run-off throughout, and by the time of the season finale, most reliability issues that are going to surface across a season have typically already done so on cars that make it this far. Safety car frequency (≈0.3) and tyre degradation (≈2/5) are both moderate, and rain is a non-factor given the UAE desert climate (rain_race_frequency ≈ 0.0).

Being the final round of the season means this circuit's rows often carry extra significance for championship-outcome context even though the reference table itself only captures track characteristics — a driver's recent form and season-long track-form features going into Abu Dhabi reflect a full season's accumulated data, more than at any earlier round.
