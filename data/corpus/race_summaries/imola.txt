Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari (Imola) — Circuit Notes

Imola is a fast, old-school permanent circuit with very few genuine overtaking zones — most of the lap is flowing, high-commitment corners taken in quick succession, with only a couple of real braking-zone passing opportunities (notably into Tamburello and the Variante Alta chicane). That's why overtaking_difficulty is rated high here (≈0.75), closer to Hungaroring and Zandvoort than to a power circuit like Monza. Grid position and clean qualifying pace matter disproportionately at tracks like this, because a driver who gets stuck behind a slower car often has nowhere to pass until the car ahead makes a mistake.

Tyre degradation is moderate-to-high (≈3/5) given the sustained high-speed direction changes, and rain shows up here more than at the desert/Gulf rounds (rain_race_frequency ≈ 0.15) — Imola sits in a part of Europe with genuinely unpredictable spring weather, and a wet or drying track at this circuit tends to scramble the grid more than usual precisely because overtaking is already hard in the dry, so drivers who gain track position in mixed conditions are much harder to repass once it dries.

Safety car frequency (≈0.5) and DNF rate (≈0.15) are both on the higher side for a permanent (non-street) circuit — the lack of run-off in several places (this remains one of the more old-fashioned tracks on the calendar) means an off-track excursion is more likely to end a car's race than at a modern Tilke-designed layout.
