Marina Bay Street Circuit (Singapore) — Circuit Notes, Pre- and Post-2023

Marina Bay is a genuine outlier in the reference table: it has the highest safety_car_frequency (≈0.9) and pit_lane_loss_time (≈24s) of any circuit on the calendar, and one of the highest DNF rates (≈0.2), reflecting a tight, humid, floodlit street circuit run at night with walls close to the racing line for almost the entire lap. Overtaking difficulty is rated high (≈0.8) in both configuration eras — this remains a circuit where track position, once established, is very hard to lose to a straightforward on-track pass.

The circuit is one of only two in the reference table (alongside Silverstone/Spa-type era changes generally) with a genuine layout change tracked as separate configuration eras: the pre-2023 layout included the Turn 16-19 chicane section around the old Singapore Sling area, which was removed for the 2023 race onward, simplifying that part of the lap and dropping the braking-zone count from 11 to 10 per lap. This is the calendar's clearest example of why the circuit reference table is keyed by configuration era rather than circuit name alone — treating pre- and post-2023 Marina Bay as identical would ignore a real, physical change to the track that a driver and car have to adapt to.

Given the combination of extreme heat/humidity, a bumpy street surface, and a high safety-car rate, races here are among the least "position stays static" of any hard-to-overtake circuit — not because passing is easy, but because incidents and cautions reshuffle the order more than at almost any other track.
