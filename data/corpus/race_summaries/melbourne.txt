Albert Park Circuit (Melbourne) — Circuit Notes

Albert Park is run on public roads around a park lake, which is why it's flagged as a street-type circuit in the reference table (is_street_circuit = 1), even though it's wider and more flowing than a tight downtown street track like Monaco or Baku. Since the circuit's significant 2022 reprofiling (walls pushed back, several corners widened and reshaped specifically to encourage racing), overtaking difficulty here sits at a genuinely moderate level (≈0.5) rather than the much harder rating the old, tighter layout would have earned — this is a good example of a circuit where a real physical change should shift how much weight grid position carries relative to older seasons at the same track.

Safety car frequency is moderate (≈0.5) — the lakeside walls are unforgiving if a car gets it wrong, but the reprofiled corners give more margin than before. DNF rate is a fairly typical ≈0.12. Tyre degradation is on the low side (≈2/5), consistent with a track that rewards a smooth, flowing driving style over one that punishes the tyres through sustained high-lateral-load corners.

As an early-season race (often the second or third round), Melbourne shares Bahrain's characteristic of reflecting pre-season and prior-year form more than mid-season in-year development trends.
